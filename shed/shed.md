## Layout Note
*   The project consists of the **Shed (10')** on the left, followed by two **Pergolas (12' each)** on the right, for a total footprint width of 34 feet (excluding roof overshoots).
*   The roof uses 12' corrugated panels centered front-to-back, resulting in approximately 23.6" overhang on the front and back relative to the wall line.
*   The total roof width is 174" (14.5 feet), covered by 8 panels with a standard ~2" overlap.
*   Pergola posts start at altitude 0.
